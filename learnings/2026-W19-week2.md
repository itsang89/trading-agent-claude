## New Rules Extracted (4)

### Execution Patterns

**Rule 1** [W2|HIGH] — Fractional stop order platform constraint.
- Alpaca does not support GTC stop orders for fractional-share positions. All stop enforcement for any fractional-share position is manual and must occur at every routine. This is a persistent platform constraint, not a transient tool error. When checking stops, verify whether stop_order_id is present in position-highs.json — if absent, enforce manually using avg_entry × 0.92.
- Context: 10+ consecutive stop order placement failures across all fractional positions (AAPL, AMZN, GOOGL, LLY, QQQ, NVDA) throughout Weeks 1–2; only XLE (whole shares) had a functioning standing stop via stop_order_id 9c12ad6b.

**Rule 2** [W2|MEDIUM] — NEUTRAL RS at mid-session: WATCH only.
- When a held position shows RS_spread in the NEUTRAL zone [−1%, 0%] at mid-session, classify as WATCH — do NOT apply the RS session-1 trim rule (which triggers only at NEGATIVE, RS < −1%). Mid-session NEUTRAL is frequently an intraday artifact; require EOD confirmation before escalating to NEGATIVE flag. This prevents false trims on recoverable positions.
- Context: 5/8 mid-session LLY RS_spread −0.215% correctly classified as WATCH (NEUTRAL, not NEGATIVE); EOD recovered to +3.994%. False trim avoided.

### Market Regime Awareness

**Rule 3** [W2|MEDIUM] — Cash deployment in BULL regime: prioritize scaling in session 1.
- When regime confirms BULL (≥8/12 universe tickers BULLISH) and multiple held positions are undersized for their conviction tier, scale ALL eligible undersized positions in that SAME session — do not spread scaling across 3–4 sessions. The BULL regime cash target (10–25%) is a binding constraint: document explicitly why each undersized position is not scaled if cash remains > 35% in session 1 of BULL confirmation.
- Context: Cash was 43.6% after regime confirmed BULL on 5/7 (Day 1); residual cash after available adds (QQQ only) was 38.85%. Primary driver of −1.808 pp weekly underperformance vs SPY (+2.368%).

**Rule 4** [W2|MEDIUM] — Commodity ETF exits: news can front-run technical signals.
- When holding a commodity ETF (XLE, XLV) and news confirms a macro catalyst reversal (e.g., oil price drop, geopolitical risk premium unwinding), treat this as a secondary signal that front-runs the likely technical trend break. Monitor intraday more closely and prepare for a mid-session exit. News does NOT override signals — but if intraday close drops below SMA at mid-session check, execute immediately rather than waiting for EOD confirmation.
- Context: 5/5 pre-market news confirmed oil price decline and ceasefire talks (Iran geopolitical risk unwind); XLE pre-market dropped from $59.45 to $57.14 (−3.88%). 5/6 mid-session XLE close $56.935 < SMA_14 $57.440 — sold immediately. Correct call; holding further would have worsened loss (5/7 close $56.98, 5/8 close $55.96).
